Three layout directions for practice

The current shell is one long column: an eight-control header, setup above the board, the transport below it, and every panel pushing the page down. These directions keep the identity and the tokens, but move the board to the center and the primary action into reach.

Header overloadEight controls compete with the drill: connect, Session, Progress, Stage, Lights, Mic, Calibrate.
Setup above the boardTabs, four selects and chips sit between the top of the page and the thing you are practising.
Board and action apartStart drill lives below the board inside a nine-button transport row. On a phone it is mid-page.
Panels push the pageSession, Progress, Calibration and Learn expand above main, moving the board and losing your place.
Flat hierarchyFamily picker, hint strip, chart, transport and setup all read at one weight; the eye has no first stop.

Direction A Desktop-first

Split studio

Three working lanes: browse on the left, the board in the middle, and everything that explains or controls the pass in a persistent coach rail.

Structural change The single column becomes a three-zone workspace, and the transport sits directly under the board instead of below a stack of setup rows.

What moves

  • The header keeps the brand, connection state, due count and a menu. Stage, lights, mic, calibration and Progress move into the menu.
  • Tabs and content selectors become a library rail; scale chips stay with the library, not above the board.
  • The board owns the center; Start/Pause and tempo sit in a transport bar attached to it.
  • The family picker, hint strip, next target, session block and Learn move into the right rail.
  • Progress, Session, Calibration and Learn open in the rail slot, so the board never moves.

Responsive

  • The library and coach rails disappear below 900px; a compact top bar and a bottom dock take over.
  • The board scrolls horizontally inside its own frame; the page never scrolls sideways.
  • Details and setup open as a bottom sheet over the board, not as new page height.

Tradeoff

The rail is excellent at 1280px and wider. On a tablet it has to collapse; on a phone it becomes a sheet, so the layout has two real modes to maintain.

Use it for

A desktop practice session where you want setup, guidance and the next target visible without scrolling away from the neck.

Recommendation

Ship Today as the home, Focus stage as the drill, and the split studio from 1024px up.

The three directions are not three competing skins. Today answers “what should I do now”; Focus stage answers “let me play”; the split studio is what Focus becomes when there is room for context beside the neck.

First five moves

  1. Cut the header to brand, status, due count and menu.
  2. Put the board before setup; move setup into a library rail or sheet.
  3. Attach the transport to the board and make one action filled.
  4. Turn Session, Progress, Calibration and Learn into drawers, not page height.
  5. Add a dedicated practice route with a bottom-docked transport.

Do not lose

The warm paper/wood identity. The accent stays reserved for the primary action and the root note. The four-level ladder. Hints, pass review and the no-light pass remain exactly where they are conceptually. Screen-only work. Every drill still runs with no guitar and no mic; the new layout must not make hardware feel required.
