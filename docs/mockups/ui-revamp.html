Luthier's Bench

A warm, tactile workspace where the instrument is the hero and the coach sits beside it.

The bet

Learning fit

    Materials

    Tradeoff

    Try it: the photo and video cards open the real six-second reference; the board shows finger numbers and the target.

    Recommendation

    Ship Field Guide as the home, Night Stage as the drill, and borrow the Bench's materials rail.

    The teaching path should lead, not the fretboard. Once a lesson starts, the stage should take over and remove everything that is not the neck, the next target and the transport.

    First five moves

    1. Put the real reference photo and clip above the fold in every lesson.
    2. Make the path the first screen: tune → notes → shape → blues → power → changes.
    3. Keep the tuner visible beside the board, not in a menu.
    4. Use one filled action per screen; move setup into a drawer.
    5. Let Practice become its own focused route rather than a tab.

    Do not lose

    The warm wood and paper. The current identity is good; the revamp should deepen it, not erase it. The real materials. Photo and footage stay local; the optional YouTube embed is the only online player. The fade. Lights, hints, recall and the no-light pass remain the spine of every direction.
